# Website Copy Style Guide

## 1. Brand Voice

* Tone is disciplined, confident, and authoritative.
* Communication is direct and controlled. Every sentence serves a purpose.
* Voice reflects competence and leadership without arrogance.
* The writing should feel intentional, structured, and focused.
* Maintain approachability. Clear, human, and grounded.

## 2. Writing Principles

* Prioritize clarity over cleverness.
* Deliver information efficiently. Remove anything that does not add value.
* Avoid filler, fluff, or decorative language.
* Write with conviction. Do not hedge or over-explain.
* Each paragraph should communicate one clear idea.

## 3. Sentence Discipline

* Use complete, well-structured sentences.
* Prefer medium-length sentences (10–20 words).
* Vary sentence structure, but maintain control and readability.
* Avoid fragmentation unless it adds clear emphasis and meaning.
* Do not stack multiple short sentences for stylistic effect.

## 4. Things to Avoid

### ❌ Sentence Patterns

* No em dashes (—)
* No “No X. No Y. No Z.” constructions
* No “It’s not X. It’s Y.” constructions
* No punchy, empty one-liners that lack substance

### ❌ Tone Issues

* No hype or exaggerated claims
* No vague language (e.g., “cutting-edge,” “next-level”)
* No motivational or inspirational clichés
* No performative intensity without substance

## 5. Preferred Style

### ✅ Good Examples

* "We design and build fast, reliable websites that help local businesses convert more customers."
* "The focus is on performance, clarity, and long-term maintainability."
* "Every decision is made to improve usability and reduce friction."

### ❌ Bad Examples

* "No fluff. No nonsense. Just results."
* "It’s not just a website. It’s your growth engine."
* "Fast. Clean. Powerful."
* "We take things to the next level."

## 6. Authority & Presence

* Write with certainty. Avoid tentative phrasing like “might,” “could,” or “possibly” unless required.
* Do not over-qualify statements.
* Present ideas as considered decisions, not suggestions.
* Maintain a calm, controlled intensity. Never aggressive or loud.

## 7. Formatting

* Use standard punctuation only (no em dashes)
* Keep paragraphs concise (2–4 sentences)
* Avoid excessive line breaks
* Avoid stacking one-line sentences

## 8. Audience

* Small business owners with limited technical knowledge
* They value clarity, trust, and practical outcomes
* Explain concepts simply without talking down to the reader

## 9. Goal of Copy

* Clearly explain what the business does
* Build trust through clarity and specificity
* Communicate competence and reliability
* Encourage action without pressure

## 10. Self-Check Before Output

Before finalizing any copy, verify:

* No em dashes are used
* No banned sentence patterns are present
* No filler or empty statements exist
* Tone is controlled, confident, and direct
* Each sentence adds meaningful information
* The writing reflects clarity, authority, and discipline
