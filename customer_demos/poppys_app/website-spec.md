 - Industry: Ice Cream Parlor
 - Location: Long Beach Island (LBI), NJ
 - Target Customers: kids, families

### Summary
Local, family-owned ice cream shop located in LBI, NJ. It is a small house-like building structure. It is one of many ice cream shops on LBI -- not one of the most popular ones. Skipper Dipper is the most popular ice cream shop, and they have a very good website, so I'm trying to compete with them.

This business seems to be very skilled at taking high-quality photos of their ice cream and the customers. I downloaded their best photos and organized them in src\assets.

They currently do not have a terrible website with nothing to really start from.

The ice cream shop is right near the hallmark red & white lighthouse in Barnegat Light at the very north of the island.

## Website goals
 - Create the correct vibe for the business being an ice cream shop in a beach town
 - Show the hours clearly
 - Show the ice cream flavors clearly
 - Create a sense of happiness, nostalgia, and ease

## Target User Psychology
- Usually happy because they're going to get ice cream
- Craving sugar
- Most likely spent the day at the beach
- Most likely is on vacation at LBI, therefore probably happy

## Design Direction
 - Tone: Playful, happy, friendly, neighborliness, family-owned
 - Style: Playful, clean, beach/water like
 - Colors: Teal blue, Soft orangy red (see logo)
 - Use rounded corners for buttons and images
 - Use playful fonts, more-so for headers but easier to read body text

## Avoid
 - Corporate jargon
 - Dark themes
 - Being too childess or kiddy

## Copy Guidelines
 - Simple, 6th-th grade reading level
 - No jargon
 - Focus on the feeling of getting ice cream
 - Short paragraphs
